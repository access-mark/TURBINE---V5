What adoption means

Discovery maps the existing operating model, followed by an agreed business blueprint, data mapping, permissions, workflow configuration, validation and team adoption. The goal is to operate the business on TURBINE, not preserve a permanent patchwork of applications.

Availability and specialist requirements

Sector-specific statutory, regulated, safety-critical, payroll, clinical, tax and other specialist requirements must be assessed and independently qualified before any production claim or deployment. The website does not imply certification or general availability.

INDUSTRY OPERATING MODEL

How independent professionals operates on TURBINE

One-person business administration. TURBINE is intended to be the primary environment for this full operating cycle, not a layer joining separate industry applications.

Business records and daily work

Enquiries, client records, quotations, appointments, task lists, expense capture, invoices, receipts, document history and a simple owner view of cash and commitments.

People and accountability

Owner-operator, with external accountant or occasional assistant where relevant

Illustrative business scenario

A sole proprietor receives an enquiry, quotes the job, schedules delivery, records costs, issues an invoice, follows up payment and reviews what needs attention tomorrow.

Owner and management questions

What has been promised? What is due? Which work is blocked? What has been purchased? Which customer amounts are outstanding? Which decisions require approval? Each answer should trace back to a controlled business record.

End-to-end business journey

  1. Discover and qualify the customer requirement
  2. Create and approve the commercial commitment
  3. Plan work, people, purchasing and any relevant stock
  4. Execute and document the agreed delivery
  5. Invoice, collect and reconcile the financial outcome
  6. Review exceptions, profitability and next priorities

Exact screens, automations and specialist workflows depend on qualified functionality and the approved organisation blueprint.

Adoption rather than integration

During discovery, existing customer, supplier, work and financial data are assessed for migration through DataPort. The business then configures its operating rules and brings users into one TURBINE environment. External banking and statutory infrastructure may remain necessary, but a permanent patchwork of business applications is not the target model.

Product scope illustration — not a representation that all industry-specific functions have passed production qualification. Clinical records, regulated industry functions and statutory compliance are not implied.
