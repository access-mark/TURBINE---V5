ADOPTION BY BUSINESS SCALE

The whole operating system, configured for your business

Business size changes complexity, permissions and workflows—not the underlying proposition. TURBINE remains the primary operating environment.

Sole proprietor and freelancer

Capture leads, quote accurately, schedule work, keep expenses, issue invoices, record receipts and see upcoming obligations. The objective is one owner-controlled record of the entire business, not multiple subscriptions.

Worked scenario: An independent designer wins a branding assignment: enquiry → proposal → acceptance → tasks → delivery → invoice → receipt → follow-up.

Small team and microbusiness

Introduce shared customer ownership, work assignment, supplier purchasing, stock if needed, approval checkpoints and basic financial control without losing visibility.

Worked scenario: A five-person repair firm allocates a customer job, approves a parts purchase, assigns a technician, records completion and bills the customer.

Growing SME

Standardise roles, handovers, purchasing authority, customer and supplier data, project delivery, receivables and management reviews.

Worked scenario: A 35-person technology services company coordinates a multi-site deployment from opportunity through equipment procurement, service delivery and staged billing.

Established and multi-team business

Use organisational permissions, delegated authority, common operational records, exception reporting and documented financial controls across teams. Multi-entity specifics require qualification.

Worked scenario: A distributor coordinates sales, warehouse and finance against the same customer order, including partial fulfilment and outstanding balances.
